Why this exists

We're surrounded by contacts — and starved of connection

The tools tell you what: who changed jobs, who raised money. They never tell you why someone gets up in the morning, or what they actually need right now. That's the gap.

Signal overload

Plenty of "interest". Not enough clarity on what matters, when it matters, and who it matters to.

Low-trust outreach

Too much cold noise. People ignore messages that don't feel human, timely or relevant — and reputations pay for it.

Good people don't meet

The right organisations and the right partners don't always find each other — or don't move fast once they do.

How it works

A simple loop, designed for change

Match, message, move. Every step creates evidence — what worked, what didn't, and what should happen next.

01

Signal

Spot intent and hypotheses before any human contact — who's worth understanding, and why.

02

Spark

Make a warm introduction, or a high-quality first outreach that respects the reader's time.

03

Align

Confirm the fit: outcomes, constraints and expectations — so nothing drifts.

04

Connect

Run the right conversation and capture what actually matters about the relationship.

05

Adapt

Learn and improve — matching, messaging and next steps get sharper every cycle.

The longer it runs, the more the intelligence compounds — not lock-in through friction, but value that exists nowhere else.
