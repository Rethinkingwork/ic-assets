Three things, done properly

Most "people strategies" stall because they treat recruiting, retaining and developing as separate problems. They're one continuous relationship — and that's where the value lives.

01 — Recruit

The right people

Not just CVs that fit a role — people whose motivations fit the mission. Twenty-five years of search says the ones who thrive were connected, not just qualified.

02 — Retain

Keep them, genuinely

People stay where they feel included, recognised and trusted. We help leaders build the conditions that make good people want to stay — not perks, but real connection.

03 — Realise

Unleash the potential

The whole point. Galvanise someone's intrinsic motivation and one plus one makes more than two. We help you coach for awareness, not just performance.

Teams outperform companies. Always have. Always will.

Teams care about each other; org charts don't. The best performance comes from mutual accountability, shared purpose and genuine human connection — and that's exactly what most leaders are taught to overlook. Rethinking Work is about putting it back.

The human behind it

Twenty-five years of helping people find their place

Rethinking Work is led by John Bennett — recruiter, HR practitioner and coach, who's spent his career placing people into organisations and watching what makes them thrive or struggle. The pattern was always the same: the ones who flew were connected; the ones who failed were under-connected, not under-qualified.

The work is grounded in a genuine, caring interest in people — who they are, what drives them, and what's getting in their way. No bollocks, no corporate speak. Just honest conversation that helps leaders and their people do their best work.
