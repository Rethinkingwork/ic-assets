CIS / Scout — round four

Ten glyphs, conventional first

Geometric bones, optically softened terminals and apertures, and the wing used as a module only where it earns its place. The 180° rotation stays the construction grammar for genuine counterparts — Log sent / Log reply, and Chat — and is not forced on symbols that already have a settled meaning.

01The rules this round runs on

Your ruling, restated as production constraints so the drawings can be checked against them.

Read

Open or receptive geometry, predominantly outline, movement inward. No solid marker anywhere in the glyph.

Write / spend

A closed destination or committed terminal plus one solid orange consequence marker. Survives monochrome as the only filled mass in the glyph.

The ring

Only where a context is genuinely held, bounded or switched. It appears once in this round, in Workspace switcher A.

Selected tab

Ground tint plus a deep-teal underline, carried by the container. Never orange, so it cannot be read as a write action.

One motif each

Each glyph carries a single metaphor. Where the wing appears it is a corner, a terminal or a speech form, not a second idea layered on the first.

Credit balance

Drawn as status. Orange is held back for the warning state, shown at the end of section 02.

02The family

Each glyph at 24px and at actual 16px, colour beside monochrome. Alternatives are offered only for Draft, Credit balance and Workspace switcher.

03Silhouettes without labels

The same set at 16px with nothing to help it. Top row is the ten resolved glyphs in order; bottom row is the four already-drawn actions they sit beside in the panel, for silhouette spacing. Those four are unrevised round-three drawings, and checking them here surfaced a real fault: in the round-three file the wing in Log sent and Log reply was scaled and translated outside the 24px box, so only its tail was rendering. It is corrected in this row, and those two need redrawing properly alongside whichever alternatives you pick.

04Selected tab, without orange

Six tabs at panel scale. Selection is ground plus underline; the glyph itself does not change colour, so the write grammar stays unambiguous.

05Collision audit at 16px

Pairs that could be confused in monochrome at 16px, and the silhouette difference that separates them. Shown as the eye actually meets them: small, mono, adjacent.

Two things I did not resolve for youCredit balance is drawn as an icon here, but it works better as a component: meter plus numeral, where the numeral is the primary element and the meter is 14–16px beside it. Both alternatives are shown in that context at the end of the family. Workspace switcher B spends its whole silhouette on the rotation, which means it no longer contains a wing at all.
